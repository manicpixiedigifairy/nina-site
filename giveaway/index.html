01 · About the project

About The Project

As part of this creative exchange project, I'll help make over your portfolio or make your first site. It's a design-based project, so it's ideal for creatives and entrepreneurs looking to showcase their work.

✓A good fit

Creatives and entrepreneurs who need a first portfolio site or a makeover of an existing one, and are looking for support with the design, the professional narrative strategy behind it, or both.

×Not a fit

Involved web development projects. Custom code is available as part of the service, but builds that need an involved backend or CMS are evaluated case by case.

05 · Questions

FAQ

Does it cost anything?

No. The site is free as part of this creative exchange project.

Who is this for?

Creatives and entrepreneurs who want to showcase their work, whether you need your first portfolio site or a makeover of the one you have.

What kinds of projects aren't a fit?

This is a design-based project, so it isn't suitable for involved web development projects.

Do I need to know what I want?

No. Share your vision if you have one. If you don't, we'll shape it together during the process.

What platform will my site be built on?

Your site is a custom build, so it can be deployed on almost any major web hosting platform, including WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, Readymag, Universe, Figma Sites, Cargo, Format and Canva (with limited functions).

Who owns the site and the copy?

You do. You'll get a final copy of all necessary working files, and you'll own and manage the site after launch.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. Your domain and hosting aren't included, but your build is made to deploy on the platform you choose.

How long does it take?

Timelines vary based on project needs, but the goal is one month to six weeks max from intake to launch.

What happens to the information I send?

It goes privately to Nina and is only used to review your application and get in touch. It's never published.
