Decree I: The Assignment

Build Momentum Fit For A King

The mandate was to build cultural momentum and online excitement in the weeks leading up to the March 2021 premiere, then convert that awareness into premiere week viewership.

I developed and executed organic and paid social strategy across Instagram, X, Facebook and TikTok, optimizing platform specific creative formats, community engagement and paid amplification.

Decree II: The Insight

A Kingdom, Not A Release Date

We treated the IP as a living universe rather than a movie to be marketed. If Zamunda were real, it would have a royal feed, daily rituals, a fashion house and a palace that talks back. Every post was written from inside the kingdom.

“Coming 2 America is a quintessential film in pop culture, as such, we needed a campaign at the intersection of marketing and culture.”

Ukonwa OjoGlobal Chief Marketing Officer, Prime Video and Amazon Studios

Decree III: The Palace Feed

Live From The Palace

@ZamundaRoyals launched on Instagram and Twitter from the point of view of the royal family's own live feeds. Daily Zamundan horoscopes, royal declarations issued from the palace, in-world lifestyle and fashion content, and shareable quizzes, filters and prompts asked fans to engage as citizens of the kingdom.

Decree VI: Royal Engagement

No Citizen Left On Read

Round the clock community management kept the world of the royal family intact. Reactive and proactive, the palace prioritized high profile moments with celebrities and key influencers and answered its citizens in character.

Talent and influencers shared creative on their own channels and amplified owned messages. Scripted self shoot prompts let talent create assets from the safety of home, and social toolkits guided content creation across the court.

Decree VII: The Coronation Night

#RoyalWatchParty

On premiere day, a virtual watch party made up for the feeling of a communal night at the theater and gave the premiere its own trending moment.

Hosted on Twitter by icon Dionne Warwick and supported by high visibility talent and influencer partners, the palace led real time content and engagement through a live tweet of the film, then kept the celebration going with an Instagram Live after party with Nigerian singer and songwriter Tiwa Savage. Detailed toolkits gave influencers, cast and hosts copy and assets to share before, during and after.

The watch party generated higher engagement than any previous Prime Video movie.
