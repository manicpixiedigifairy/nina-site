01The Brief

Who and What

The Client

Who

eBay, the online marketplace carving out a distinct lane in sneaker culture by celebrating Black sneaker history, building collector loyalty, and setting itself apart from competitors like StockX through authenticity and cultural storytelling.

The Campaign

What

Opened ‘The ’85 Shop’ pop-up in Chicago to pay homage to the Air Jordan 1’s 1985 birth and its role in modern sneaker culture, featuring a complete collection of original Air Jordan 1s in every colorway, a never-released sample, era sneakers like Puma Clyde and Adidas Forum, memorabilia from the Air homage film, and modern retro offerings, complemented by a 10-day online auction of featured items.

02Cultural Impact

Why It Matters

This unique sneaker exhibit tackled the underrecognition of Black sneaker origins by immersing visitors in the Air Jordan 1’s transformative history, countered exclusivity in resale markets with accessible celebrations, and positioned eBay as a heritage curator in the modern era of sneaker culture.
