01 The Brief

The Kalahari Melon & Aloe Vera launch was Mielle's first major launch under the P&G umbrella. With everyone watching, we did something that had never been done before. We made women with 4c hair the aspirational hero of the whole campaign, and reframed natural hair around joy, play and ease.

Why It MattersHealthy, moisturized hair unlocks creativity and confidence. The campaign treated moisture as freedom, not maintenance.

02 The Big Idea

Imagine What You Could Do In 5 Days

One wash day. Five days of moisture. Five chances to switch it up. The product truth became the whole creative engine.

01

Wash Day, Starting Five

Shampoo, two minute deep conditioner, leave in, weightless oil and curl glaze. Used as a system, moisture locks in for up to five days.

02

Switch It Up Daily

Wash and go on Monday. Bantu knots by Wednesday. Carefree curls for the weekend. Every day is a new look, not a new wash.

03

Share The Glow

Friends, followers and neighbors all get the put on. The joy is contagious, and so is the content.

03 The Spots

Mielle's First National TV Spot After P&G

Two 30 second films with one truth at the center. Moisture that lasts gives you room to live. The dialogue was written insider first, so an inter generational audience would hear itself and laugh.

Spot OneThe Switch Up

A naturalista's five day hair journey. Sleek wash and go, playful bantu knots, carefree curls, each look matched to the day's adventure and cheered on by her followers. A light hearted demo of the KMA Effect: every day is a chance to switch it up.

Spot TwoThe Put On

Two neighbors on opposite ends of the haircare spectrum. One struggles with dry, frizzy hair. The other exudes confidence with hydrated, bouncy curls. One hallway encounter and a life saving put on later, the hero discovers the joy of long lasting moisture across shower moments, yoga class and nights out.

05 The 5 Day Challenge

An Advent Box For Your Hair

To get influencers excited for launch, we built a hands on challenge so they could feel the Kalahari Melon and Aloe Vera difference first hand. The custom PR box was a five day interactive experience: each day opened a new styling challenge and a premium surprise gift.

Creators immersed themselves in the line and invited their followers to join in with their own five day vlog style content. Tap a day to open it.
