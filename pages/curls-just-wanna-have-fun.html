It starts with one wash day

The Ask

This was Mielle's first major launch under the P&G umbrella, and everyone was watching. The brief was to make KMA the go to solution for type 4 hair and connect it to a bigger story about personal agency and self expression.

The Insight

Used as a system, the collection locks in moisture for up to five days. One wash day becomes five days of healthy, moisturized hair. Literally, every naturalista's dream.

The Way In

We made women with 4c hair the aspirational hero of a national campaign, positioning Kalahari Melon & Aloe Vera as the key to the freedom of time and the power of uncompromising style.

Hero spot

The Switch Up

A naturalista's five day hair journey, told look by look. Sleek wash and go, playful bantu knots, carefree curls, each matched to the day's adventure and cheered on by her followers. A light hearted demo of the KMA Effect: every day is a chance to switch it up.
