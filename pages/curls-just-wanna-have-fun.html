Editor's Note

The Kalahari Melon & Aloe Vera launch was Mielle's first major launch under the P&G umbrella. With everyone watching, we did something that had never been done before. We made women with 4c hair the aspirational hero of a national campaign and reframed natural hair around joy, play and ease.

The product truth was simple. Used as a system, the collection locks in moisture for up to five days. So we asked one question and built everything around it: imagine what you could do in 5 days?

This lookbook is the answer. One wash day, then five days of switching it up.
