The Insight and The Idea

The Insight

The lore of chef life has taken over pop culture, but Black chefs are not represented in the dominant narrative.

The Idea

Hero Black chefs as the main characters of the campaign, recognizing them as the skilled culinary craftsmen they are.

03The Creative Engine

For Foodie Consideration

Real nominations from foodies, talent, influencers and past Royals become custom digital posters, each spotlighting a chef the community loves.

The posters are shared across Dig In, influencer and restaurant partner channels, rolling out as weekly in-feed carousels that introduce every nominee to a new audience and remind foodies to nominate.

  • In-Feed Carousel Announcement
  • Interactive Announcement Story
  • Branded GIPHY Sticker Pack
04Mobilize the Foodie Community

Reintroducing Royalty

To activate foodies, the program hits three beats: educate them on how it works, help them spot the royals in their community, and get them excited to nominate. Rich storytelling from the restaurateur community grounds why it matters.

Six micro influencers lend their voices, each recording green screen videos. One explains how to nominate. The next shows love to a chef they consider royal.

05Sustain and Reveal

Keep the Crown Shining

Launch Stunt

The Blue Knight

A mascot in blue armor turns up at foodie events and Black owned restaurants, unidentified, until influencers lift their helmets and share the royal decree that Restaurant Royalty has returned.

Royal Reveal
