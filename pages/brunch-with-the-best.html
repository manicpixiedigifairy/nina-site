01The Territory

The Insight and The Idea

The Insight

Brunch is a sacred occasion for chosen family, gossip and nourishment in Black culture.

The Idea

Put regional brunch cultures head to head online, then unite them in one menu with chef backstories that nourish community beyond food.

  • Social
  • Digital
  • In Person

Pepsi Dig In is PepsiCo’s platform empowering Black-owned restaurants. Brunch With The Best culminates its Restaurant Royalty residency, amplifying Black culinary talent and cultivating long-term restaurant partnerships. The activation satisfies the craving for meaningful Black food content.

02Dual Plane Activation

One Menu, Two Planes

The Royal chefs take the campaign off the feed and onto the Las Vegas Strip. Pepsi Dig In stages a brunch takeover with top Black chefs and a mixologist from across the country, each serving signature dishes, to celebrate the way Black foodies and chefs have rebranded brunch as an art form.

Online and in person run as one dual plane activation. Engagement stories send people to a real table, and the moments captured on site feed the next posts. The two planes keep passing the energy back and forth across three beats.

03Interactive Owned Content

Head to Head, Vote by Vote

On Pepsi Dig In’s own channels, regional brunch cultures went head to head and the community did the deciding. Votes, polls, questions and a countdown turned every story into a pick, a conversation or a plan.

The templates carried the same doodle world and Brunch With The Best wordmark as the room, so every tap felt like part of the table. Each engagement story pointed back to the takeover with a tagged location, a reservation link and “meet me there gameday weekend” copy.

04Chef Moments

Six Chefs, Told Twice

Each Royal feature gets a day in the spotlight, first as a sweepstakes story and then, after the takeover, as a behind-the-scenes meme. The chef moments keep the brunch going long after the last plate.

Amplification

Sweepstakes Stories

Every Royal chef gets a personalized graphic to share on Instagram stories, hyping their audience for the sweepstakes and the takeover.

Echo

Chef Life Memes

On site, the crew captures the realities of chef life during brunch service. After the event, the clips come back as meme-format Reels and TikToks that Royals share on their own feeds.

Spread the Word

Calling All Black Restaurateurs

A one-sheet for Royals to post on their stories opens the movement to every Black-owned restaurant. The ask is a Reel showing why they are number one at brunch: show the dishes, introduce the staff or share a limited-time brunch special.

It is the dual plane idea in a single frame. Restaurants post online to get on the map, and the guides send people to their real tables.
