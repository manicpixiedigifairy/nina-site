01The Idea

Crown the Restaurateurs

The Insight

Brunch is one of the biggest casual dining occasions in the country, and the restaurateurs behind it set the table for how people gather.

The Idea

Close the Restaurant Royalty residency with a Brunch Royale that crowns the restaurateurs pushing the brunch tradition forward.

With Better With Pepsi already putting the meal first and Pepsi right beside it, brunch was the natural next table. Every piece of the program was built around restaurateurs: their signature dishes on one menu, their stories on Pepsi Dig In’s channels, and local brunch spots in the spotlight.

Around The Dig In Table
To some, brunch is just another meal. Around the Dig In table, it’s much more. It’s an occasion, a reason to gather, a chance to gossip, and a momentary getaway from the world outside your circle. It’s where chosen family comes to be fed in a way that food alone can’t satisfy. It’s a kind of nourishment that takes years of tradition, taste tests, and trash talk to truly understand. For us, community is everything. And this is the space we built to get together and make each of the chefs pushing the brunch tradition forward feel like royalty.
02The Takeover

One Menu, Two Planes

Online

Head to Head

Brunch culture from across the country went head to head on Pepsi Dig In’s social channels, with chef stories, interviews and creator reviews feeding the conversation.

In Person

One Culturally Infused Menu

Chefs and a mixologist from across the country brought their signature dishes to one menu at a brunch takeover at Della’s Kitchen in Las Vegas, February 8 to 10.
