Nina AustinMethodology

MyCreativeDNA

My signature is the system behind the story.

I’ve earned a reputation as a master architect of brand worlds, designing social-first ecosystems where a brand’s values become a point of view people choose to follow.

01

Approach

02
No. 01

A real idea can carry a system.

I know a campaign tag or concept works when its language can hold an entire platform: clear rules, a repeatable narrative spine, and a defined voice. That architecture is what lets one big idea extend across TVC, creator partnerships, and brand-led experiences without losing the thread.

03
No. 02

The audience is the co-author.

Brands no longer dictate the narrative alone. From product R&D to the comments section, the community shapes the story in real time. I write work with open ends: formats people can remix, lines they can repurpose, gaps they want to fill.

04
No. 03

Human truth, built to scale.

I translate specs and brand ideals into one human sentence. A feature list tells people what a product can do. Brand values sell them on a promise. The work has to show what it adds to someone’s day. Once that truth is clear, attention and consideration follow.

05
No. 04

Strategy is the engine.

The strongest creative is rooted in a real command of the category, the brand’s equity, and the audience’s mindset. I partner with strategy from before the initial brief all the way through the final review, so the insight drives the idea instead of decorating it.

06
No. 05

Social is not a lesser craft.

This is a hill I live on: traditional first principles and social savvy aren’t opposites. A six-second vertical still needs a sharp insight. Work made for social spaces doesn’t have to be trivial. I hold a TikTok to the same standard as a broadcast spot and shape it for the way people watch.

07

Method

08
Step 01

Deconstruct the digital behavior.

I start where the category creates friction. I study how people already engage with it across platforms: the search queries, the comment threads, the stitches, the creator content they consume. Identifying how people actually behave is a cheat code. It hands the work an insider POV with genuine potential to resonate.

09
Step 02

Define the semiotics.

I identify the visual and verbal codes that tell an audience the work was made for them. Whether the strategy calls for high-camp irony or minimalist utility, I specify the cues precisely because belonging is signaled in details.

10
Step 03

Build the environment.

I structure a campaign the way a product team structures an app. I map the journey from the first algorithmic hook to the deepest editorial or episodic content, and I assign each layer a job: stop the scroll, entertain or educate, deepen the relationship.

11
Step 04

Disrupt with intent.

Every bold idea is anchored in an audience or data insight. I break one convention at a time, so the risk is specific, defensible, and measurable.

12
Step 05

Ideate format first.

In social-first work, the feed is the native habitat. I lean into visual grammar that lands with the sound off and script language people type into search. Social-first is the shape of the idea from day one, not a cutdown at the end.

13
Step 06

Design for earned.

The strongest campaigns outgrow their original placements. I give every idea a reason to travel: a line worth quoting or a format worth duplicating. When people carry the work beyond the media plan, the brand gains credibility no budget can buy.
