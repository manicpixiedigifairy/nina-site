Chapter I: The Occasion

A Century Of Crunch Calls For Old Friends

Butterfinger turned 100 in 2024. For a bar that got its name from a sportscaster’s word for players who couldn’t hold onto the ball, the birthday needed a guest who could hold a room.

So the brand brought back The Simpsons, the family that fronted Butterfinger from the late 1980s into the early 2000s, and put them on limited-edition wrappers with confetti, balloons and a Celebrating 100 Years shield.

Evolve with modern culture to reach new audiences.

Neal Finkler, VP of Marketing, Butterfinger, on honoring the brand’s legacy at 100
