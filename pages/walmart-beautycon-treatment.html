The Manifesto

The days when being an it girl meant “wearing your worth” are finally over. Today, the true it girl knows exclusivity is the least original way to stand out.

Real originality is taking something anyone can have and making it uniquely your own. It’s knowing how to use a six-dollar eyeshadow palette to create a runway-worthy smoky eye. It’s having the vision to transform an everyday oversized t-shirt into a trendy two-piece set. It’s knowing how to look past the price and see the value in everything around you.

And when you’re looking for an affordable foundation in your shade or self-care that doesn’t cost you all your coin, Walmart’s beauty aisles have always been the place to turn. Whether it’s the most affordable long-wear lip liner ever or the newest wash day remedy, the girls that get it, get “it” at Walmart.

The place for every it girl.

The Slayground05 / 09
Walmart Makers Studio . Beautycon LA . 2023

Welcome To The Slayground.

Walmart’s haven of beauty exploration, where attendees played with new beauty trends, new products and new friends, no pressure. Familiar beauty scenes, from the at-home vanity to getting ready with friends for a night out, were reimagined into a contemporary beauty play space built for next-generation audience engagement.

Walmart Makers Studio is a multi-seasonal experiential campaign giving emerging Gen Z creators creative resources and chances to collaborate. Its third edition, at Beautycon LA, turned Walmart’s beauty offerings into a run of interactive moments, made to be tried, shared and shopped.
