07Troubleshooting

If You Run Into Problems

Issue 1

It Feels Unaffordable

In 2024, 92% of enrollees received advance premium tax credits, averaging $536 a month, and 44% of consumers selected plans costing $10 or less a month.

Issue 2

It Is Only For New Enrollees

Not quite. 76% of 2024 selections were returning or renewing consumers, while 24%, about 5 million people, were new.

Issue 3

My State Has Its Own Site

That is expected. 32 states use HealthCare.gov, while 18 states plus D.C. run State-Based Marketplaces, with nearly 4.9 million selections between them.
