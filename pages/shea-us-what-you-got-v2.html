What You Got

A tropical trivia-style game show that launched the Curl Stretch Collection in the language of curly girl culture, and gave a skeptical audience a reason to show up for the brand again.

Social StrategyScriptwritingOriginal FormatCampaign VoiceInfluencer Marketing

The
Problem

Post acquisition, SheaMoisture needed a way to rebuild trust with a more skeptical audience.

SheaMoisture, a Unilever-owned personal care brand focused on textured hair, seeking to rebrand and reinforce authenticity amid waning support.

The
Solution

A digital trivia-style game show inspired by family game nights to promote the new Curl Stretch Collection, using classic elements of curly girl culture to articulate product benefits from an insider’s point of view.

Distributed across digital platforms, leading with Instagram and adding TikTok for interactivity, boosted with endorsements from influencers.

By speaking the language of the consumer, we drove buzz and re-ignited the brand’s base.

The Formula

Why It
Worked

The format gave people a reason to watch a product video to the end. By building the claims into a game, the copy made Curl Stretch’s benefits memorable and shareable, and gave the brand a repeatable franchise it could extend across creators and cut-downs.

(1)

Entertainment-first structure that still delivered every product proof point.

(2)

Modular one-liners and categories designed to scale across a full content slate.

(3)

A format editors could name and recommend, earned credibility beyond the view counts.

The Copy
Did The Selling

The format had to teach the product without sounding like an ad. I translated each Curl Stretch benefit into a game category and a two-part tension question, so the science (anti-shrinkage, length, detangling, Kalahari melon) landed as comedy and competition, not a spec sheet.

(1)

Categories engineered around claims:

Bring Me The MoistureThe Secret SauceBlack Hair EssentialsHang Time
(2)

Two-part host questions with an IYKYK comedy beat baked in, answered by the product truth.

Shrinkage had you down bad,but what changed?

Asked on the show

(3)

Franchise one-liners for reuse across cut-downs:

Curly Q’sAll Tea, No Shea’dThe Mane Stage
