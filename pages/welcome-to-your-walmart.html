Aisle One: The Shift

From Everything To Everything For You

The Challenge

One of the world's largest retailers had become so ubiquitous it was bordering on invisible. With a new personalized app experience launching, Walmart was ready to evolve.

The Insight

The campaign was built on a simple but powerful shift. Walmart isn't the store that has everything for everyone. It's the store that has exactly what you love, need, and can't wait to try, all at the push of a button.

The Execution

The work reintroduced Walmart as a digital-first shopping destination, inviting consumers to see the app as their personalized Walmart: in-store, curbside, home delivery, and Walmart+.
