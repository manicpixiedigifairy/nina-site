Insight:

Women want to feel good in every inch of their skin, not just their face.

The Brief

Every Inch.
Even the Skipped Ones.

1

Who

Suave, a mass-market personal care brand historically associated with budget basics, launching a new Advanced Skin Solutions line and looking to reposition as a credible body care authority without abandoning its accessible roots.

2

What

Grounded in the insight that women want to feel good in every inch of their skin, the campaign reframed Suave as the proud sponsor of every body part people skip, using the visual language of prestige skincare with wit and warmth.

3

Where

Social-first, leading on Instagram and TikTok, with #SponsoredBySuave built as a participatory hashtag. A hybrid UGC and influencer model paired brand-directed creator content with an open invitation to tag your own overlooked spots.

4

Why It Matters

Body care has long defaulted to aspirational fantasy. This campaign met consumers in the reality of their skin, building a UGC mechanic into the concept itself so participation became its foundation, not merely a best-case outcome.

The Creative Concept

Neglected Body Parts

Built to launch Suave's Advanced Skin Solutions body lotion lineup, the Calming Relief and moisturizing collections, the strategy ran entirely on satire and humor. Often-overlooked zones, from crusty elbows to dry knees, the back of the neck and the tops of the thighs, became individual personalities finally stepping into the spotlight. Creators joked about their body parts entering their "influencer era" or landing a "knee deal" via Suave.

  • Humor-first and satirical by design, never a straight product demo.
  • The product became the "deal" that got each body part its moment of fame.
  • Phrasing was repeatable, remixable, and inherently shareable.

Aesthetic & Framing

Honest Close-Ups

Instead of heavily edited studio productions, lifestyle and beauty creators delivered native, casual GRWM and situational-comedy clips that fit their existing feeds. Across Instagram Reels and TikTok, assets featured zoomed-in, un-retouched closeups of dry skin changing into hydrated skin, the same honest register as the comedy and proof the product works.
