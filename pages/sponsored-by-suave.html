The Brief

Every Inch.
Even the Skipped Ones.

Who

Suave, a mass-market personal care brand historically associated with budget basics, launching a new Advanced Skin Solutions line and looking to reposition as a credible body care authority without abandoning its accessible roots.

What

Grounded in an insight that women want to feel good in every inch of their skin, not just their face, the campaign reframed Suave as the proud sponsor of every body part people consistently skip. From the back of the neck to the inner thigh, each execution adopted the visual language of prestige skincare while reclaiming overlooked skin with wit and warmth.

Where

Built as a social-first campaign leading on Instagram and TikTok, with #SponsoredBySuave designed from the ground up as a participatory hashtag. A hybrid UGC and influencer model paired brand-directed creator content with an open invitation for consumers to tag their own overlooked spots and join the sponsorship, extending organic reach well beyond paid media inside a 360 campaign ecosystem.

Why It Matters

Body care has long defaulted to aspirational fantasy. This campaign met consumers in the reality of their skin care experience. By building a UGC mechanic into the concept itself, participation became a foundation of the campaign rather than a best-case outcome.

The Creative Concept

Neglected Body Parts

Built to launch Suave's Advanced Skin Solutions body lotion lineup, the Calming Relief and moisturizing collections, the strategy ran entirely on satire and humor. Often-overlooked skincare zones, from crusty elbows to dry knees, the back of the neck and the tops of the thighs, became individual personalities finally stepping into the spotlight.

Creators joked about their body parts entering their "influencer era" or landing a "knee deal" via Suave.

  • Humor-first and satirical by design, never a straight product demo.
  • The product became the "deal" that got each body part its moment of fame.
  • Phrasing was repeatable, remixable, and inherently shareable.
Creator Integration
Aesthetic & Framing

Honest Close-Ups

Instead of heavily edited studio productions, lifestyle and beauty creators delivered native, casual GRWM and situational-comedy clips that fit their existing feeds. Across Instagram Reels and TikTok, assets uniformly featured zoomed-in, un-retouched closeups of dry skin changing into hydrated skin, the same honest register as the comedy and proof the product works.

"my driest zones finally got sponsored"
Recurring on-screen text overlay

Every laugh had a destination. Content carried direct retail intent toward Walmart for the Calming Relief lotion, connecting the joke to a concrete place to buy.
