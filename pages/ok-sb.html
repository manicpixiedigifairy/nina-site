01The Overview: Mission And Method
Mission

Take advantage of the biggest national sports event and media holiday of the year, during one of the rare years AT&T was not a sponsor.

Method

AT&T handed out free, gloriously mediocre sports merch outside the big football event in Atlanta, to show how absurd settling for just OK really is.

All without ever using the words “Super Bowl.”

The stand inspired a collection of merch built on big game vagueisms. Who wouldn’t want that?

04Postgame: The Recap

Two Days. One Very OK Takeover.

AT&T was not an official sponsor of the game that year, with no access to official NFL messaging, logos, or any other property.

It did not matter. Across two days we tapped into the national attention and the staggering foot traffic around the city to deliver our hilariously OK message.

Nobody should settle for OK. Not on a shirt. Not on a network.
