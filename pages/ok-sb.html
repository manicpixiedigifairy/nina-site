01Pregame: The Setup

Nobody settles for just OK at the big game.Ridiculous on a souvenir. Strangely normal on a phone plan.

The Super Bowl is not an average football game. It is the biggest football event of the year, so fans expect every part of it to be the best, down to the souvenirs they carry home to prove they were there.

So we built the opposite. An “OK” merch stand giving out “OK” sports gear with “OK” messages, exactly what an average experience at the biggest sporting event of the year would look like. Nobody would accept it here. Plenty of people accept it from their wireless service every day.

  2. The Standard

    The Souvenir

    The shirt, the hat, the thing you hand a friend to prove you were there. It had better be great.

  3. The Settle

    Just OK

    The same shrug people give their wireless service, printed on a T-shirt where it finally looks as silly as it is.

04Postgame: The Recap

Two Days. One Very OK Takeover.

AT&T was not an official sponsor of the game that year, with no access to official NFL messaging, logos, or any other property.

It did not matter. Across two days we tapped into the national attention and the staggering foot traffic around the city to deliver our hilariously OK message.

Nobody should settle for OK. Not on a shirt. Not on a network.
