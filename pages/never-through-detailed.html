[ 04 ]Frame 04: The Strategy

Heart, On And Off The Field

Cultural Context

When it comes to sport, we tend to think of heart as something akin to grit and determination, but it's more than that. Athletes with heart break records and barriers. They change organizations and communities. They create dynasties and movements.

Heart is the intersection of humanity and human performance. Today's athletes are on the front lines of social justice movements, pandemic relief efforts and everything in between. Heart is the key to on-field performance, but its true measure is off-field progress.

The Target: Focused Performers

The Focused Performer is motivated and driven. They want to perform at their best, always, on and off the playing field. They want sportswear brands that operate with the same kind of heart as them, brands with values and the conviction to back it up. Brands that don't just deliver on their functional needs, but emotional needs too.

Leading The Pulse

2021 continues to show us the interconnectedness of sports and culture. There is no delineation, and there won't be in 2022. What happens off the playing field affects what happens on the playing field. If Under Armour helps athletes in their pursuit of making the world better, Under Armour makes sports better.

For Under Armour to truly be credible as a purpose-driven brand, we can't just tell Focused Performers how our brand will make them better. We also need them to emotionally invest and subscribe to why our brand will make their world better.

Diversity In Sport

Theme: Challenging Racism

Black and brown athletes carry sports culture and get no support in their unique struggles on and off the court. Players end up feeling exploited and out of place in the world of sports.

Playing Fair

Under Armour levels the playing field for Black athletes with scholarships that take the cost out of getting in the game.

The Tension
Nearly 1 in 5 lower-income parents say costs forced their kids to cut back on sports, and 86% of student athletes live in poverty. For athletes from minority groups, price is the first opponent.
The Move
A nontraditional scholarship that covers the non-academic, sports-related expenses, so money never interrupts a Focused Performer mid-pursuit.
StoryboardHow it plays out
Frame 01Need-Based Scholarships

Campus partners across the country, maybe the world, fund an entire class of 2022 athletes.

Frame 02Beyond Sport Stipends

Tuition, then everything tuition misses: travel to games, uniforms, equipment, laundry, food.

Beat
02 / 06

Playing Fair, Extended

Under Armour takes on exploitative NCAA regulations with the California government at its back.

The Tension
56% of Division I men's basketball players and 48% of football student-athletes are Black. The top 20 NCAA colleges clear over $3 billion a year from their programs.
The Move
Push California's Fair Pay To Play Act into federal law so athletes in all 50 states can profit from their name, and work with Overtime Elite to pay players a salary from high school on.
StoryboardHow it plays out
Frame 01Collective For Change

Lobbyists, lawyers, marketers and key athlete partners, assembled as one team for reform.

Frame 02Crunching The Numbers

Total up what the former student athletes on the roster lost, and spend that exact figure on the awareness campaign.

Frame 03Taking The Floor

Former NCAA athletes on the roster speak before Congress for the next generation.

Bridging The Gap

Under Armour launches an apprenticeship that empowers athletes from under-represented communities to change the makeup of sports.

The Tension
In 2020 the NFL opened the season with three Black head coaches and two Black general managers, in a league whose players are roughly 70% Black. Athletes who don't go pro drop out of sport entirely.
The Move
A groundbreaking initiative that shows high school athletes every career the industry holds beyond the field.

Breaking The Binary

Under Armour creates the first-ever gender neutral locker room and starts to break the binary for college athletes.

The Tension
Trans athletes have competed on NCAA teams consistent with their gender identity for nine years. The buildings never caught up.

The first gender neutral locker rooms in colleges across the country, with the conversation and the community to match.

Beat
04 / 04

Fit For Every Body

Under Armour pulls the labels off its clothes and lets athletes identify however they want while they shop.

The Tension
56% of Gen Z already shop outside their gender, ignoring the labels and the gendered aisles.
The Move
A major push to make product accessible across the gender spectrum, and to drag the industry's norms along with it.
StoryboardHow it plays out
Frame 01Beyond Labels

In the spirit of the Curry 6 and Riley Morrison, labels come off the site and the aisles, and unisex jerseys and the non-gender-specific Meridian line go in.

Gender non-conforming and non-binary designers build the line: breathable chest binder tops, biker shorts with built-in tucking gaff, swim trunks without netting.
