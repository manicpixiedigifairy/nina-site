[ 05 ]Frame 05: The Strategy

Heart, On And Off The Field

Cultural Context

When it comes to sport, we tend to think of heart as something akin to grit and determination, but it's more than that. Athletes with heart break records and barriers. They change organizations and communities. They create dynasties and movements.

Heart is the intersection of humanity and human performance. Today's athletes are on the front lines of social justice movements, pandemic relief efforts and everything in between. Heart is the key to on-field performance, but its true measure is off-field progress.

The Target: Focused Performers

The Focused Performer is motivated and driven. They want to perform at their best, always, on and off the playing field. They want sportswear brands that operate with the same kind of heart as them, brands with values and the conviction to back it up. Brands that don't just deliver on their functional needs, but emotional needs too.

Leading The Pulse

2021 continues to show us the interconnectedness of sports and culture. There is no delineation, and there won't be in 2022. What happens off the playing field affects what happens on the playing field. If Under Armour helps athletes in their pursuit of making the world better, Under Armour makes sports better.

For Under Armour to truly be credible as a purpose-driven brand, we can't just tell Focused Performers how our brand will make them better. We also need them to emotionally invest and subscribe to why our brand will make their world better.

Phase 01

Diversity In Sport

Q1
Black History Month, Super Bowl and Playoffs

Universal Insight

Black and brown athletes contribute so much to sports culture, but don't receive support in their unique struggles on and off the court. As a result, players end up feeling exploited and out of place in the world of sports.

Theme: Challenging Racism

MediaDiversity In Sport FilmBuzz: the brand actions below
Brand Action 01

Playing Fair

Under Armour levels the playing field for Black athletes with scholarships that alleviate the financial burden of getting in the game.

Insight
In 2019 the poverty rate was 18.8% for Black people and 15.7% for Hispanic people, against 9.6% for white people. Nearly 1 in 5 lower-income parents report costs forced their children to cut back on sports, and 86% of student athletes live in poverty. Cost acts as a major barrier to entry for athletes from minority groups.
Idea
A nontraditional scholarship program that covers non-academic, sports-related expenses, so financial obstacles don't interrupt our Focused Performers in their pursuit of progress.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Need-Based ScholarshipsWork with campus partners around the country, maybe even globally, to offer need-based scholarships to an entire class of 2022 athletes.
  • 02
    Beyond Sport StipendsIn addition to tuition, offset non-academic costs like travel to and from games, uniforms and equipment, and general cost of living.
Brand Action 02

Playing Fair, Extended

Under Armour is challenging exploitative regulations in the NCAA with the backing of the California government.

Insight
56% of Division I men's college basketball players and 48% of football student-athletes are Black, yet the top 20 NCAA colleges collectively make over $3 billion a year from their athletic programs.
Idea
California's Fair Pay To Play Act, signed in 2019, lets college athletes profit from endorsements. We'll lobby to make it federal law so athletes in all 50 states benefit, and work with Overtime Elite to provide players a salary and benefits from high school on.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Collective For ChangeAn original team of lobbyists, lawyers, marketers and key athlete partners leading the charge for reform.
  • 02
    Crunching The NumbersCalculate the total amount lost by the former student athletes on our roster and donate that amount to launch an awareness campaign around the issue.
  • 03
    Taking The FloorFormer NCAA athletes on our roster speak before Congress and advocate for the next generation.
Brand Action 03

Bridging The Gap

Under Armour launches an apprenticeship program to empower athletes from under-represented communities to change the makeup of sports.

Insight
In 2020 the 32 NFL teams started the season with only three Black head coaches and two Black general managers, in a league where the players are roughly 70% Black. The disparity leads athletes who don't make it professionally to drop out of sport altogether.
Idea
Address the racial disparity between Black players and Black leadership by creating a groundbreaking initiative exposing high school athletes to alternative career paths in the sports industry.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    See It. Be It.An apprenticeship program with high schools across the country, in partnership with IMG Academy, exposing young sport enthusiasts to sports-adjacent careers.
  • 02
    Becoming A Major PlayerBring in the crucial team members who work for our athletes off the court, managers, agents, contract lawyers, as mentors.
  • 03
    Leaders In TrainingSponsor college curriculum on the path to these careers with special guest hosts like Deion Sanders from leadership positions in the industry.
Brand Action 04

Passing The Mic

In an industry that often tells athletes to shut up, Under Armour is giving them a platform to speak up about issues that matter.

Insight
People who thought kneeling during the anthem was appropriate rose from 32% in September 2016 to 41% in September 2017 to 48% in July 2020. There's a growing appetite, within the athlete community and among fans, for athletes to own their politics.
Idea
Organize a collective of the top athletes on our roster to ideate on ways Under Armour can change the systemic issues in the sports industry and push the game forward.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Speaking OutLaunch with a statement of purpose describing the goals the team will organize around and a list of demands to the industry.
  • 02
    A Seat At The TableLive roundtable discussions moderated by sports journalists on the need for change in sport.
  • 03
    Teamwork Makes The Dream WorkThe collective informs Under Armour's initiatives over five years, applying a collaborative product innovation strategy to athletes' needs.
Brand Action 05

Getting In The Game

Under Armour champions athletes from underrepresented groups as the new face of the most niche sports out there.

Insight
Certain overwhelmingly white sports often forcefully exclude non-white athletes from participating, reinforcing the idea that there are only certain sports Black and brown athletes can take part in. The MLB is almost 60% white.
Idea
Confront the lack of diverse representation in traditionally white sports by sponsoring young, star athletes from underrepresented groups paving their own way, starting with our inroads in baseball and expanding to sports of all kinds.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Star Player SpotlightSponsor a wave of up-and-coming BIPOC athletes in traditionally white sports and empower them as the new face of those sports.
  • 02
    Voices Of The Next GenerationAn awareness campaign with content and a live speaker series at schools and community centers, where brand ambassadors speak about the exclusion they've faced.
  • 03
    Making SpacePartner with community centers in underserved neighborhoods to provide coaching, free equipment and safe spaces to explore more sports.
Brand Action 06

A Timeless Fight

Under Armour examines the longstanding history of sports in protest to empower the next generation to keep taking a stand.

Insight
Protest has always played a pivotal role in the world of sports, and Under Armour has a history of its athletes using their platform for change, including Steph Curry, Aisha Praught-Leer, Andy Murray and Natasha Hastings.
Idea
Honor the legacy of sports activism through an in-depth documentary, empowering the next generation of athletes and activists and highlighting the rich history of protest in sport, including the ongoing legacy of our own athletes.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Protest Through The AgesAn in-depth documentary on the ways sports activism has generated results and shifted the landscape of the industry over time.
  • 02
    Looking Back To Move ForwardFormer sports activists and current leaders facilitate discussion and strategize for athletes looking to use their voice: how to engage your team in organized action, how to deal with backlash.
  • 03
    Funding The FutureProceeds from the release go back to ongoing initiatives to reform the industry, with input from the athletes featured in the film.
Phase 02

Gender In Sport

Q1 to Q2
International Women's Day, March Madness

Universal Insight

In a recent study, 41% of Gen Z participants said they identify as neutral on the spectrum of masculinity and femininity. Outdated gender norms in sports exclude and limit today's generation of athletes from participating in and bettering themselves through sport.

Theme: Tackling Gender

MediaGender In Sport FilmBuzz: the brand actions below
Brand Action 01

Mom VP

Moms make some of the greatest athletes, and Under Armour is stepping up to make sure they can stay in the game.

Insight
NCAA experts suggest that pregnancy rates for female student-athletes are as high as 10% to 15%.
Idea
Show up for college athletes who are moms, and future moms, in a way that validates the value they bring to sport, helping them through pregnancy, postpartum recovery and the return to the game.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Providing Tools For ProgressNear Mother's Day, partner with major stadiums and brands like Medela to equip sporting facilities with nursing rooms and postpartum essentials.
  • 02
    Through It All PartnershipsCommit to financially sponsoring the new mom athletes on our roster during and post maternity leave.
  • 03
    Recovery RedefinedA maternity and postpartum athletic wear line that accommodates a new mother's needs and eases the transition back into being active.
Brand Action 02

Lifting The Weight

Under Armour is taking the pressure of being othered in the gym off the shoulders of non-binary athletes.

Insight
According to a 2015 study, nearly 1 in 5 transgender people said they were denied equal treatment or service, verbally harassed, or physically attacked at a gym within the past year because of being transgender.
Idea
Partner with and elevate gyms designed specifically to provide safe spaces for queer, trans and gender non-conforming college athletes.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Creating CommunityIn the days leading up to Pride, an event at The Queer Gym, the first dedicated LGBTQ gym in the U.S., with Steph Curry as host.
  • 02
    Transformation ToolkitsPartner with gyms across the country to transform them into more inclusive places for non-binary athletes.
  • 03
    Doing The Heavy LiftingGuidance from sports D&I experts like Athlete Ally to help with the transformation of these spaces.
Brand Action 03

Breaking The Binary

Under Armour creates the first-ever gender neutral locker room to start to break the gender binary for college athletes.

Insight
For nine years, transgender athletes at NCAA member universities have been able to compete on teams consistent with their gender identities. Despite that, accommodations haven't been made to empower trans athletes at the college level to succeed.
Idea
Support athletes across the gender spectrum by creating the first-ever gender neutral locker rooms in colleges across the country.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Starting The ConversationA podcast featuring top athletes in conversation about the evolution of gender norms in sports.
  • 02
    Power In NumbersVirtual support groups for gender non-conforming high school athletes to experience community and support each other.
Brand Action 04

Fit For Every Body

Under Armour removes all the labels from its clothes and empowers athletes to identify however they want while they shop.

Insight
56% of Gen Z consumers already shop outside of their gender, or cross-buy, ignoring clothing labels and gendered sections.
Idea
A major push to make sports more inclusive by making our products more accessible to those across the gender spectrum, pushing industry norms to serve all Focused Performers equally.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Beyond LabelsIn the spirit of the Curry 6 and Riley Morrison in 2019, remove labels from our site and aisles, and add unisex products like jerseys and a non-gender-specific Meridian line.
  • 02
    Leading By ExampleCompile a comprehensive research report and share it with competitors to create a new industry standard of gender inclusivity.
  • 03
    Designing A New DayWork with gender non-conforming and non-binary designers on an intentional, informed product line: breathable chest binder tops, biker shorts with built-in tucking gaff, swim trunks without netting.

Beyond Sport

Q2 to Jun 2022
Mother's Day, Pride

Universal Insight

Theme: Beyond Sport

MediaBeyond Sport FilmBuzz: the brand actions below
Brand Action 01

Off Court Excellence

Under Armour celebrates the off court excellence of Seahawks wide receiver and aspiring chef DK Metcalf.

Insight
Athletes are often looked at as one dimensional and perceived as less valuable off the court.
Idea
Celebrate the lives of professional athletes outside of their role as players through a content series that highlights their pursuits outside of sport.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Second Success StoriesWell-known, high-performance athletes discuss how they apply a Focused Performer mentality outside of sport in an ESPN 30 for 30 docuseries.
  • 02
    Bigger Than Sport SeriesA recurring TikTok Live segment on athletes applying what they learned in sport to a new context, hosted by an athlete who embodies that journey, The Rock.
  • 03
    The Beginning Of BetterA Do Better, Be Better program that empowers and guides young athletes to do good in their communities off the field.
Brand Action 02

Sport Scholars

Under Armour provides ten-year scholarships to competitive athletes that demonstrate excellence off the court.

Insight
Athletes are rewarded for an almost unhealthy obsession with sports, whereas athletes who shine in off-the-court pursuits aren't given the same opportunities.
Idea
Demonstrate a long-term commitment to the whole athlete by offering ten-year scholarships to up-and-coming athletes who demonstrate excellence in off-the-court pursuits.
  • How It Works
  • 01
    Next Step ScholarshipsSupport up-and-coming athletes as they pursue the next phase of their education and athletic career, developing as well-rounded individuals.
  • 02
    Foundation For SuccessA mentorship program pairing rising star athletes with pros at the top of their game, with resources to identify and realize off-the-court dreams.
