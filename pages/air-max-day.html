01The moment

35 years of visible Air

Air Max Day lands every March 26, the day the Air Max 1 released in 1987. The 2022 edition marked 35 years of visible Air.

Nike celebrated with regional Air Max 1 drops, the Blueprint in North America, La Ville-Lumière in Europe and Wabi-Sabi across Asia-Pacific and Latin America, plus a Give Fresh Air game show on the SNKRS app.

Leading up to March 26, Nike’s channels spotlighted its collaboration with Boston boutique Concepts.

02The idea

More than a shoe

Air Max is more than a shoe. It’s a memory people carry. So the work handed the mic to the people who wear it, asked for their first memory of Nike Air, and played their answers back in the feed.

“Middle school, my mother worked hard to get me my first pair of Air Max 90.”

On Air, the designer

Juliana Sagat

A footwear designer from Paris, raised in Saint-Ouen and trained at Studio Bercot, who came to Nike in Portland after Marc Jacobs, Kenzo, Givenchy and Isabel Marant.

For the Air Max 1’s 35th, she spoke to Nike’s design shift toward women, community-driven insight and tactile, exaggerated tooling, like the double-stacked Air of the Air Max Furyosa.

Show us your Air

Your City

UGC on @nikesportswear

“Show Us Your Air” turned Nike’s feeds into a multi-platform gallery. On March 26, community content tags, curated with local boutiques and Nike partners, invited fans to post their collections, unboxings and on-foot grids. Their looks were repurposed into city-tagged posts that sent people to shop the collection on Instagram.

Air Max Day Worldwide

One Show 2022 · Bronze · Digital Design

The second edition built on the first. Air Max Day Worldwide, the first entirely virtual Air Max Day, won a One Show Bronze for its 2021 launch. With in-person events off the table, it brought Nike’s local celebrations online as a members-only, 24-hour experience: timed events from fifteen Nike geos, a live stream from the Nike Campus, a 3D tour of an athlete’s sneaker closet, and a digital passport that stamped every city a visitor reached.
