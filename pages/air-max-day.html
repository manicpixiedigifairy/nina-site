The case03 / 26

01

The moment

Air Max Day lands every March 26, the day the Air Max 1 released in 1987. The 2022 edition marked 35 years of visible Air.

That made the milestone a story about time as much as a shoe: a first pair, a hand-me-down, a sneaker you still remember wearing to school.

02

The idea

Air Max is more than a shoe. It’s a memory people carry. So the work handed the mic to the people who wear it, asked for their first memory of Nike Air, and played their answers back in the feed.

“Middle school, my mother worked hard to get me my first pair of Air Max 90.”
03

The rollout

Five films, from 13 to 51 seconds, cut for vertical and 4:5 feeds.

A run of Instagram Stories that moved from an open question, to a quiz on Tinker Hatfield’s original sketches, to quick Air facts and a style tip for the 1987 Air Max 1.
