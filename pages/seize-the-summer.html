Who

Walmart, the global retail leader, set out to re-engage a new generation of explorers and lifestyle-driven consumers, tapping into the resurgence of travel and adventure in the post-pandemic era.

02 / The Campaign

What

We launched the “Seize The Summer” adventure series, a video content program profiling inspirational talents like pro surfer Hunter Jones and travel creator N’Dea Irvin-Choy, sharing their stories of outdoor exploration to encourage young audiences to embrace activities such as surfing, hiking, and solo travel.

  • Surfing
  • Hiking
  • Solo travel
03 / Channels

Where

Distributed via video installments on digital platforms like Hypebeast and Blavity. The full-length content also lived on Walmart’s owned YouTube channel, while cut downs were distributed as paid social assets across platforms.

04 / Cultural Impact

Why It Matters

A conduit to adventure

The series tackles underrepresentation in outdoor adventures, where Black and marginalized groups face barriers like lack of visibility, safety concerns, and accessibility.

By spotlighting diverse stories, the campaign helped foster the spirit of discovery aligning making Walmart a conduit to adventure.

  • Lack of visibility
  • Safety concerns
  • Accessibility
05 / Creative Lead

My Role

As creative lead, I crafted the narratives for each episode and developed the treatment for the docu-style capture of each video profile.

Each interview guide was designed to balance authentic capture of talent’s voice and organic infusion of brand messaging.

The outcome were relatable stories of personal triumphs, perseverance, packaged to resonate with Gen Z.

Partner 02

Blavity

Program: Wonder-Lust With Us x Walmart

The challengeWalmart wanted to grow awareness of its Black and Unlimited program with African American Gen Z and Millennial audiences. Heading into summer, the focus turned to outdoor adventure, a space that has historically left Black explorers out.

The approachBlavity built a custom program around its travel vertical: the annual Travel Noire Awards, three outdoorsy influencers, and five co-branded editorials on the importance of being outside, with products to help you be prepared.
