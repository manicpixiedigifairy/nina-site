Course One

To Start

Lightly seasoned with Better With Pepsi, the platform that put the meal first and Pepsi right beside it.

The InsightStrategy

Brunch is one of the biggest casual dining occasions in the country, and the restaurateurs behind it set the table for how people gather.

The IdeaConcept

Close the Restaurant Royalty residency with a Brunch Royale that crowns the restaurateurs pushing the brunch tradition forward.

Around This Table

To some, brunch is just another meal. Around this table, it’s much more. It’s an occasion, a reason to gather, a chance to gossip, and a momentary getaway from the world outside your circle. It’s where chosen family comes to be fed in a way that food alone can’t satisfy. It’s a kind of nourishment that takes years of tradition, taste tests, and trash talk to truly understand. For us, community is everything. And this is the space we built to get together and make each of the chefs pushing the brunch tradition forward feel like royalty.

Course Four

Table Setting

Working with production partners across Pepsi’s IAT, we translated the momentum on social into a conceptually aligned in-person experience, designed so the room itself would keep traveling organically.

ArrivalDesign

Larger than life chef portraits and Brunch With The Best signage carried the look of the feed into the hallway, with the same reserve your seat call to action guests had seen on social.

The TableDesign

Branded tabletops, printed menus and a custom cocktail card put every chef’s signature dishes and drinks in front of every guest.

The BarDesign

A Pepsi bar at the center of the room, with the mixologist behind it, gave every toast a moment worth posting.

The BackdropDesign

Doodle walls, a mission wall and photo-ready Show Love textures turned the space into content that kept moving after the plates were cleared.
