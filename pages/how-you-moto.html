Why It Matters

Tech as an extension of self.

Cultural Impact

A phone that reflects you.

Addressed market fragmentation by positioning the Moto G as a versatile, self-expressive device, transforming tech into a reflection of individuality and driving renewed brand loyalty in a crowded smartphone market.

How It Did

A lifestyle brand, not just a tech player.

The campaign rolled out globally, revitalizing the Moto G brand with a unified message that resonated across diverse audiences. By blending personality driven storytelling with sleek product design, it reintroduced Motorola as a modern lifestyle brand.

My Role

Scripts, tagline, lens.

As a freelance Associate Creative Director, I developed each script and coined the campaign's closing line, the only right way to use it is yours, to embody personalized storytelling. I partnered closely with directors to bring a fresh, modern lens that treats recreation as part of identity.
