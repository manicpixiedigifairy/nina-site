01The Bill

Admit One To The Hustle

Admit OneThe Client

Who

Walmart, the world’s largest retailer, strategically empowering a wider student demographic during the back-to-school season with on-campus resources for academic success, creative expression, and personal resilience.

Admit OneThe Campaign

What

Launched the Black & Unlimited HBCU Tour, featuring a mobile creator studio for student entrepreneurs to showcase skills in beauty, photography, videography, design, and artisanship, learn monetization strategies, and engage in interactive booths, live DJ sets, and presentations.

Admit OneCultural Impact

Why

Invested resources into empowering Black students with tools for legacies and resilience, countering underrepresentation in entrepreneurship to translate “sparks” into long-term success.

Turning creative skills into entrepreneurial pathways, one campus parking lot at a time.

Set List

  1. 1

    HBCU Marching Band Showcases

    We collaborated with campus and alumni groups to host pop-up performances by student drumlines and dancers.

  2. 2

    Live DJ Sets + Motivational Talks

    Events were elevated with DJ sets, emcee moments, and drop-ins from community figures and alumni who shared actionable tips for personal growth and career building.

  3. 3

    Brand Discovery + Product Demos

    Students interacted with Black-owned and multicultural brands sold at Walmart, gaining exposure to entrepreneurship and learning how to monetize creativity in tangible ways.

  4. 4

    Shoppable + Shareable Social Content

    Each stop encouraged students to create and post their own content inside the mobile studio, bridging IRL experiences with digital engagement and peer amplification across platforms.
