The Brief

Who

(The Client)

Nike, a global sportswear brand, aiming to showcase consistent support for BIPOC women and empower Gen Z women athletes through mentorship and representation.

What

(The Campaign)

Launched “Come Thru,” a first-of-its-kind long-form YouTube series featuring hand-picked talent across three impact pillars: Community Catalysts, Elite Athletes, Gen Z Makers, guided by our host for real, personal conversations on issues impacting women.

Where

(Channels)

Digital activations on YouTube for main episodes (10-15 minutes each), Instagram for 0:15 ancillary paid media assets and in-app product promotion.

Why It Matters

(Cultural Impact)

Born from necessity in 2021 to create accessible, inspiring spaces for Gen Z women, addressing real issues, fostering empowerment, and changing career trajectories amid broader unprecedented societal pressures.

On Air

YouTube TV

Episodes released incrementally, making the series a form of destination media within the culture.
