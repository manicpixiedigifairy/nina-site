Who:The Client

Walmart, the global retail powerhouse seeking to strategically invest in purpose-driven marketing to address cultural consumer demands for authentic, values-based brand interactions.

Level 2

What:The Campaign

Developed and launched Dear Dad, a multifaceted Father’s Day campaign anchored by a livestream gaming event and an ABFF (American Black Film Festival) short film series. The livestream paired NFL icon Cam Newton and gaming legend IpodKingCarter with their sons in a retro-styled game lounge featuring NBA Jam, VR Racing, and other classics, while the film series offered intimate, narrative-driven portraits of Black fatherhood, written by emerging filmmakers.

Level 4

Why It Matters:Cultural Impact

PlayPrideFilm

In a media landscape where Black fatherhood is often misrepresented or invisibilized, Dear Dad reclaimed the narrative through play, pride, and film. By combining joyful, accessible moments with cinematic storytelling, Walmart strengthened emotional connection with Black audiences and reinforced its investment in long-term cultural equity.

Level 5

How It Did:Results

The livestream generated 67K+ live views, 500K+ total watch minutes, 15M+ impressions, and a 7.5x CTR. The overall campaign earned multiple accolades including a Shorty Impact Award, a Gold Shark International Award for branded content, and a 2024 Equity & Inclusion Webby Nomination, affirming its cultural and creative impact.
