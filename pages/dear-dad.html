Who:The Client

Walmart, the global retail powerhouse seeking to strategically invest in purpose-driven marketing to address cultural consumer demands for authentic, values-based brand interactions.

What:The Campaign

Developed and launched Dear Dad, a Father’s Day campaign celebrating modern fatherhood. It was anchored by a docu-style short film series and extended on TikTok through a microcreator campaign of user-generated content.

The Series

An Ode to Modern Fatherhood

We worked with Walmart to launch a series of docu-style short films to serve as both an ode to modern fatherhood and an exploration of its evolution.

The Short Film Series

Pointed intergenerational conversations directed by three emerging filmmakers. By scratching beneath the surface of the daily lives of a few real fathers, each film offered a new perspective on fatherhood today.

Film I

Legacy of the Sea

A film by Dior Rodriguez

Legacy of the Sea is a story of preserving and passing down traditions. Dior’s concept was born from her own experience with her late father, who was a maker and empowered an entire generation by sharing his wisdom.

Film II

Dear Dad

A film by Jared M. Royal

Jared’s Dear Dad takes place on Sankofa Farms and explores the story of father and farmer Kamal Bell. The result is a compelling, cinematic portrayal of a new kind of family story.

Film III

Just Breathe

A film by Rodney Rikai

Rodney’s story is a reflective, autobiographical homage to his journey as a full-time single father and uncle. As a self-titled Dunkle, Rodney has learned to juggle becoming his own man while also raising his son and nephew.

Why It Matters:Cultural Impact

By pairing cinematic storytelling with the ordinary rituals of real households, Walmart strengthened emotional connection with its audiences and reinforced its investment in long-term cultural equity.

The Business Case:Earning Affinity

The campaign filled a white space in the media landscape, building emotional trust with families and positioning Walmart as a supporting actor in the narrative of everyday life.

01

Connect With Consumers

Real fathers and families told their own stories, so shoppers saw themselves first and the brand second. Lasting affinity built on recognition of shared values rather than standard RTBs.

02

Protect Share of Voice

Competitors like Amazon have the upper hand on speed, price, and convenience. Earning a meaningful role in people’s lives defends share of voice in a way that more media spend alone does not.

03

Play the Supporting Actor

Walmart appears inside the moments that matter, from the Father’s Day gift to the family table. The families lead the story, and the brand earns its place in it.

Families Lead.Walmart Supports.

How It Did:Results

The campaign earned multiple accolades including a Shorty Impact Award, a Gold Shark International Award for branded content, and a 2024 Equity & Inclusion Webby Nomination, affirming its cultural and creative impact.
