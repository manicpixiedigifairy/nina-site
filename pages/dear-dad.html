Who:The Client

Walmart, the global retail powerhouse seeking to strategically invest in purpose-driven marketing to address cultural consumer demands for authentic, values-based brand interactions.

What:The Campaign

Developed and launched Dear Dad, a Father’s Day campaign celebrating modern fatherhood. It was anchored by a docu-style short film series and extended on TikTok through a microcreator campaign of user-generated content.

The Series

An Ode to Modern Fatherhood

We worked with Walmart to launch a series of docu-style short films to serve as both an ode to modern fatherhood and an exploration of its evolution.

By scratching beneath the surface of the daily lives of a few real fathers, each film offered a new perspective on fatherhood today. Pointed intergenerational conversations were juxtaposed with the visual narrative of intimate, everyday moments. Directed by emerging filmmakers Jared Malik Royal, Dior Rodriguez, and Rodney Rikai, the films captured authentic, nuanced stories of real dads who are shifting the narrative in their day-to-day lives.

The Short Film Series

Film I

Legacy of the Sea

A film by Dior Rodriguez

Legacy of the Sea is a story of preserving and passing down traditions. Dior’s concept was born from her own experience with her late father, who was a maker and empowered an entire generation by sharing his wisdom. This poetic story explores themes of family traditions and heritage in a way that is relatable yet refreshingly nuanced and original.

Film II

Dear Dad

A film by Jared M. Royal

Jared’s Dear Dad takes place on Sankofa Farms and explores the story of father and farmer Kamal Bell. This poetic doc explores how Kamal is using foundational knowledge in innovative new ways to create a lasting legacy. Jared drew inspiration for this theme from his own family history and the generations of fathers that paved the way for him. The result is a compelling, cinematic portrayal of a new kind of family story.

Watch the Film
Film III

Just Breathe

A film by Rodney Rikai

Rodney’s story is a reflective, autobiographical homage to his journey as a full-time single father and uncle. As a self-titled Dunkle, Rodney has learned to juggle becoming his own man while also raising his son and nephew to become the men they are meant to be. Just Breathe is a commentary on modern fatherhood and parenting styles that both draws from and departs from the models of prior generations. This refreshing new story is an inspiration for the new fathers of today to feel seen.

Why It Matters:Cultural Impact

ModernFatherhood,Every Day.

In a media landscape where modern fatherhood is often misrepresented or invisibilized, Dear Dad reclaimed the narrative through the real stories of Black fathers and everyday families. By pairing cinematic storytelling with the ordinary rituals of real households, Walmart strengthened emotional connection with its audiences and reinforced its investment in long-term cultural equity.

The Business Case:Earning Affinity

Dear Dad was built to earn affinity, not just attention. Competitors like Amazon fight for share of voice on speed, price, and convenience. Walmart can’t win that fight by shouting louder. So the campaign put real families in the lead and positioned Walmart as a supporting actor in the narrative of everyday life.

01

Connect With Consumers

Real fathers and families told their own stories, so shoppers saw themselves first and the brand second. Affinity built on recognition lasts longer than a message built on a sale.

02

Protect Share of Voice

Against competitors like Amazon, a meaningful place in the cultural conversation defends share of voice in a way that more media spend alone does not.

03

Play the Supporting Actor

Walmart appears inside the moments that matter, from the Father’s Day gift to the family table. The families lead the story, and the brand earns its place in it.

Families Lead.Walmart Supports.

How It Did:Results

The campaign earned multiple accolades including a Shorty Impact Award, a Gold Shark International Award for branded content, and a 2024 Equity & Inclusion Webby Nomination, affirming its cultural and creative impact.
