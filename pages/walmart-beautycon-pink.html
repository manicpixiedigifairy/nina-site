01 . the comeback

They called it the Super Bowl of beauty.

Beautycon, the high-profile festival that closed in 2021 amid financial turbulence, made a comeback. Reimagined under Essence Ventures, the new Beautycon was built on merging content, community and commerce.

Walmart partnered as the exclusive retail destination, amplifying diverse, rising creators and the unique beauty styles they bring to the floor. The partnership started from an emerging audience strategy: meet Gen Z and millennial beauty lovers where their next favorite products, looks and creators are found.

02 . the ethos

For Every It Girl anchored the event’s resurrection.

One creative ethos guided every touchpoint, from the floor plan to the storefront to the words on the walls. Beautycon’s bold, rebellious and inclusive identity reimagined traditional beauty standards for Gen Z and millennial audiences, and the It Girl gave Walmart its way in. Every touchpoint was built for next-generation audience engagement, made to be tried, shared and shopped.

03 . the manifesto
“The girls that get it, get ‘it’ at Walmart.”

The days when being an it girl meant “wearing your worth” are over. Today’s it girl knows exclusivity is the least original way to stand out. Real originality is taking something anyone can have and making it uniquely your own, like a six-dollar palette turned into a runway-worthy smoky eye.

Walmart’s beauty aisles have always been where she turns for a foundation in her shade or self-care that doesn’t cost all her coin. The place for every it girl.

04 . live from LA

Welcome to The Slayground.

Walmart Makers Studio . Beautycon LA 2023

Welcome to Walmart’s haven of beauty exploration, where attendees played with new beauty trends, new products and new friends, no pressure. Familiar beauty spaces and scenes, from your at-home vanity to getting ready with friends for a night out, were reimagined into a contemporary beauty play space.

From experimenting with new beauty cores to boosting content capture confidence with posing coaches, Makers Studio at Beautycon LA offered the ultimate journey to re-upping that it girl energy and confidence.

Walmart Makers Studio is a multi-seasonal experiential campaign giving emerging Gen Z creators creative resources and chances to collaborate. Its third edition showcased Walmart’s beauty offerings across a series of interactive moments. Attendees curated personalized product combinations, tried Cottagecore and Mermaidcore at AR-enhanced vanities designed by beauty creators, and worked with creator David Suh on their posing in a dedicated photo studio.

05 . the rebrand

Calling all originals.

Beautycon’s identity went bold, rebellious and inclusive, reimagining traditional beauty standards for Gen Z and millennial audiences. Color-blocked posters took the comeback to the street, from wild postings to the subway to the skyline.

06 . the feed

Calling every beauty rebel.

The renewed messaging carried the comeback into the feed: bold, rebellious, color-blocked posts calling every beauty rebel back to Beautycon. A demographic cultural engagement strategy put the diverse faces, trends and creators these audiences actually follow at the center.
