The Challenge

Brand stagnation in a market where consumers demand entertaining, relatable content.

The Idea

One thing is certain in life: someone will try to lay a finger on your Butterfinger.

But they will always fail thanks to the Butterfinger Investigators (aka BFI). They’re a task force whose sole purpose in life is to return stolen Butterfingers to their rightful owners. The BFI have been around for years, but with the addition of “EVER,” we opened the door to time travel, extending the possibilities of the BFI premise to the golden age of pirates, post-revolutionary folklore, and beyond.

Proving that no matter where you are (or when you are), nobody will lay a finger on your Butterfinger. Ever.

The Solution

Modernize the classic BFI device, and refresh the story with a built-in humorous premise.

Results

Measured Business & Creative Performance

Butterfinger leaned into classic mischief with its “Nobody lays a finger on my Butterfinger” line, updated for a Halloween setting with the Headless Horseman. The spot blends suspense-movie parody with humor. The result? Strong brand recall and solid engagement, proving that nostalgia and clever tone still resonate when refreshed for seasonal storytelling.

Result 1

Financial Reversal

Prior to launching the BFI creative universe, Butterfinger was suffering from cultural irrelevance and bleeding market share. The BFI campaign structure single-handedly turned around the portfolio from a negative double-digit decline into an initial +15% growth spike, followed by consecutive years of positive growth through late 2025/2026.

Result 2

High Ad Effectiveness Rating

According to independent ad-testing platform System1 Group, the BFI campaign repeatedly secured top marks for effectiveness. The character-driven narrative (“Hugh Dunnit” and “Ali Byes”) resulted in incredibly high “Fluency” scores, meaning audiences instantly recognized the brand and connected with the humor.

Result 3

Audience Expansion

The campaign effectively expanded the brand’s reach beyond nostalgia, successfully penetrating the highly competitive Millennial Parent (ages 20 to 30) and Gen Z markets across digital platforms like YouTube, Instagram, and Hulu.
