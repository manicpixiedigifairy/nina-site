The Campaign

The Silk Press Conference

We launched Silk Press in a Bottle with a viral Silk Press Conference, an influencer-led, branded social film in the style of a mock media briefing. The framing borrowed the visual grammar of hard news, driving curiosity and amplifying the groundbreaking feel of the launch.
