[ 04 ]Frame 04: The Brief

Who, What, Where, And Why It Matters

01

WhoThe Client

Under Armour, a global sportswear leader empowering active, competitive individuals, especially Gen Z, with the tools, products and guidance for peak performance amid post-pandemic challenges.

02

WhatWhat We Did

Developed the film concept for the 2022 global campaign Never Through, celebrating how an athletic mindset carries you through failures and restarts, in the never-ending pursuit of better.

03

WhereChannels

Designed for global rollout across digital platforms, social media and experiential activations, engaging Gen Z audiences with relatable content.

04

Why It MattersCultural Insight

Addressed the persistent on- and off-field adversities of 2020 and 2021 by building momentum on athlete resilience, fostering empowerment and brand loyalty among an ad-savvy generation demanding genuine narratives.
