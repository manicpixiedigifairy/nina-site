The ClientWho

Who

Walmart, the world’s largest retailer, strategically empowering a wider student demographic during the back-to-school season with on-campus resources for academic success, creative expression, and personal resilience.

The CampaignWhat

What

Launched the Black & Unlimited HBCU Tour, featuring a mobile creator studio for student entrepreneurs to showcase skills in beauty, photography, videography, design, and artisanship, learn monetization strategies, and engage in interactive booths, live DJ sets, and presentations.

Cultural ImpactWhy

Why

Invested resources into empowering Black students with tools for legacies and resilience, countering underrepresentation in entrepreneurship to translate “sparks” into long-term success.
