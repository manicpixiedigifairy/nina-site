01The Challenge

Standing Out In A Crowded Tech Landscape

Productivity tools all promise the same thing. Notion needed to feel less like software and more like a tactile, down-to-earth tool that helps people prioritize what is important.

Work and life no longer have a clear line between them. People wanted authentic, value driven ways to organize everything they care about, from the job to the family business.

02The Hybrid Audience

One Campaign, Three Tiers Of Audience

Notion was already loved as a personal tool. The campaign had to speak to people at home and at work at once, winning business decision makers through the everyday users who already trust the product.

Tier 1

Everyday Audiences

Self-motivated young professionals who use Notion to plan, track and organize the whole of their lives, from the job to the family business.

B2CAges 18 To 35+
Tier 2

Founders And Solopreneurs

Builders running a startup or a one person business, where work and life share the same calendar and the same tool.

Tier 3

Enterprise Decision Makers

Decision makers at growing tech companies and larger organizations, shown how Notion can fit into their teams’ working lives.
