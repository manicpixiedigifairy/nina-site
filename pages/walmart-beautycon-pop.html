Designed for the girls who still believe in beauty

The Super Bowl of beauty you never saw coming back.

They called it the Super Bowl of beauty. Beautycon, the high-profile festival that closed in 2021 amid financial turbulence, made a comeback, reimagined under Essence Ventures around merging content, community and commerce.

Walmart partnered as the exclusive retail destination, amplifying diverse, rising creators and the unique beauty styles they bring. It started from an emerging audience strategy: meet Gen Z and millennial beauty lovers where their next favorite products, looks and creators are found.

Walmart Makers Studio . Beautycon LA 2023

Welcome to The Slayground

Walmart’s haven of beauty exploration, where attendees played with new beauty trends, new products and new friends, no pressure.

Familiar beauty spaces and scenes, from your at-home vanity to getting ready with friends for a night out, were reimagined into a contemporary beauty play space. From experimenting with new beauty cores to boosting content capture confidence with posing coaches, Makers Studio at Beautycon LA offered the ultimate journey to re-upping that it girl energy and confidence.

Walmart Makers Studio is a multi-seasonal experiential campaign giving emerging Gen Z creators creative resources and chances to collaborate. Its third edition showcased Walmart’s beauty offerings across a series of interactive moments: personalized product combinations, Cottagecore and Mermaidcore at AR-enhanced vanities designed by beauty creators, and posing sessions with creator David Suh in a dedicated photo studio.

The manifesto
The girls that get it, get “it” at Walmart.

The days when being an it girl meant “wearing your worth” are over. Real originality is taking something anyone can have and making it uniquely your own, like a six-dollar palette turned into a runway-worthy smoky eye. The place for every it girl.
