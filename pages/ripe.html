The concept

the secondright before too much

Every fruit has one perfect hour. The skin gives a little under the thumb. The sweetness gets almost too loud. The air around it turns heavy and gold. ripe is a fragrance house built on that hour: the peak, and the decadent second before it tips over.

Not the orchard. The market stall at dusk, where everything is slightly too much and nobody is leaving.

    01.2 Personality and voice

    sensual, poeticand entirely assured

    01edgy

    It flirts with the line and knows exactly where it is.

    02elegant

    Plain words, placed slowly. Never in a hurry to sell.

    03sensual

    Skin, heat, juice, weight. Written like it can be touched.

    04assured

    No exclamation marks. It has nothing to prove.

    Tap where it speaks

    She is

    Indulgent, knowing, unhurried. Exaggeration is artistic here, never comedic. Fantasy over function, beauty with an undercurrent of danger.

    She is not

    Cute, clinical or wellness. No jokes, no puns on the fruit, no claims about what it does for you. It does one thing: it makes you want.

    01.4 The logo

    tanned, engravedor glowing into the fruit

    The logo is never printed on. It is a tan line left by the sun, a mark pressed into the skin, or light coming from inside. Pick a behavior, change the fruit, push the ripeness.

    1. Logo onlyNothing else is printed on the bottle. No claims, no volume, no legal. Those live on the box.
    2. Always the same84 percent of the body width, centered, sitting at 58 percent of the height. Eight bottles, one position.
    3. Never flatTanned, engraved or glowing. Never a flat print, never a sticker, never recolored to match.
    4. Lowercase onlyOne word, one hand-drawn line. Never set in capitals, never retyped in a font.
