manifesto.

Little by little, the beauty industry has redefined our understanding of luxury. Clean girl minimalism in pale beige color palettes. Euro summer in carefully kept linen. Careful, neat, like the waxy plastic fruit in an open-house fruit dish.

All the while, the richest moments of life were waiting on the other side of the mess. The sloppy, impulsive, delicious moments that start unplanned and end up unforgettable.

This brand embraces what life looks like when you trade delayed gratification for spontaneous indulgence.

section 02.

voice.

02

sensual.poetic.assured.

  • edgy.

    toys with subtext, saying without saying.

  • elegant.

    plain words, placed slowly.

  • sensual.

    skin, heat, juice, weight.

  • assured.

    nothing to prove.

writing rules.

always.

  • the name is always lowercase: ripe.
  • microcopy is short and ends with a period.
  • write with the body: peel, press, bruise, drip, skin, juice, savor, indulge.

never.

  • no exclamation marks, emoji or fruit puns.
  • no claims about what the fragrance does.
  • leave out: wellness, clean, minimalism, simplicity.
section 03.

look and feel.

03

tropical nostalgia.

the summer is remembered, not new. it takes its cues from Brazilian funk aesthetics, faded like a holiday snapshot in a drawer.

framing, people and surfaces.

  • close and cropped.
  • gold on skin.
  • quiet ground, loud fruit.
  • fruit pulled, peeled, bitten, bruised.
  • people shot like a phone in a kitchen in full sun: pores kept, skin of every shade.
  • never stock fruit on white, cheerful food photography, or anything clinical.

section 05.

logo and type.

05

the logo is a tan line.

a glowing lowercase cursive in one hand-made line, the only script in the brand. always an asset, never typed, never printed on.

  • tanned.

    a pale tan line on the fruit's skin.

  • engraved.

    pressed into the skin.

  • glowing.

    light from inside.

pale at under-ripe, full at peak, deep and hot past it.

  • logo only on the bottle. 84 percent of body width, centered, middle at 58 percent of body height.
  • never flat, never a sticker, never recolored. lowercase only.
  • clear space is the height of the letter r.
  • versions: ink, cream, gold (rare and small), cream on mango.

headlines shout, lines whisper.

extra bold, all caps, condensed, leading 0.9, one to three words a line, flat solid color. pair a heavy caps headline with a light lowercase line.
