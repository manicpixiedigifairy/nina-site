SheaMoisture

Shea Us What You Got

A tropical trivia-style game show that launched the Curl Stretch Collection in the language of curly girl culture, and gave a skeptical audience a reason to show up for the brand again.

Social StrategyScriptwritingCampaign VoiceInfluencer Marketing
The Brief
1

Who

SheaMoisture, a Unilever-owned personal care brand focused on textured hair, seeking to rebrand and reinforce authenticity amid waning support.

2

What

A digital trivia-style game show inspired by family game nights to promote the new Curl Stretch Collection, using classic elements of curly girl culture to articulate product benefits from an insider’s point of view.

3

Where

Distributed across digital platforms, leading with Instagram and adding TikTok for interactivity, boosted with endorsements from influencers.

4

Why It Matters

Post acquisition, SheaMoisture needed a way to rebuild trust with a more skeptical audience. By speaking the language of the consumer, we drove buzz and re-ignited the brand’s base.

The Format

Game Night, Curl Edition

Three contestants, one bell and a wheel of curl facts, played the way a family game night actually plays. Every question was written from an insider’s point of view, so product benefits arrived as culture before they arrived as claims.

  • The hostTrivia cards and a spinning wheel set the tone
  • The castCreators answering as themselves, on camera
  • The scoreA live scoreboard that made every answer count
