Pizza Hut

The Brief

01 · Who

The Client

One of the most recognized fast food brands on the planet, fighting to earn back their cultural relevance by bringing back one of their most beloved menu staples since 1999.

02 · What

The Campaign

We launched a fan-first organic campaign across Pizza Hut’s owned social channels that turned years of documented consumer demand into an energetic launch strategy. We seeded creator-led TikTok challenges and used Pizza Hut platforms as a hub for the audience to engage in debates and share reactions, converting loyal customers into earned mouthpieces.

03 · Where

Channels

The campaign ran across TikTok, Instagram (Reels, Stories, and In-Feed), X/Twitter, Reddit, and Pinterest. By breaking out of standard fast-food advertising silos, our multi-channel strategy compounded organic reach and engagement.

  • TikTok
  • IG Reels
  • IG Stories
  • IG In-Feed
  • X/Twitter
  • Reddit
  • Pinterest
04 · Me

My Role

As ACD, Copy, I wrote the clever, thumb-stopping copy and shaped how it traveled. Working with social strategists, I defined channel-specific strategies and tailored activations for niche digital enclaves, each grounded in platform-specific consumer behavior. With community managers, I set an effective posting cadence, established a phase roll-out strategy, and found real-world assets that affordably stretched the campaign well past its launch window.

02

Interactive Stories

Owned-channel mechanic for low-friction consumer co-creation

Interactive Stories were the campaign’s conversion engine, deployed with a clear intention: to convert passive viewers into active participants. We did this through things like:

  1. Two-option polling stickers (#TeamHold vs. #TeamFold)
  2. Sliding scale “pizza hunger level” meters
  3. Embedded tap-forward/swipe-up links
  4. Sequential “this or that” preference quizzes
  5. “Add Yours” sticker prompts
  6. Open-ended feedback boxes/stickers
